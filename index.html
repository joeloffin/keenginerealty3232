Clear. Secure. Fair.

KE Realty is a virtual land investment company that purchases land directly and works with a network of builders on homes and projects of many sizes. Closings are handled by a licensed title company or closing attorney in your state — so the process stays professional and protected from start to finish.

How it works

Three straightforward steps

No listing, showings, or unnecessary back-and-forth.

  1. 01

    Agree on the purchase

    We review the property, answer your questions, and sign a clear agreement to purchase your land.

  2. 02

    Secure the closing

    We place the agreed earnest-money deposit with a licensed title company or closing attorney in your state.

  3. 03

    Receive your payment

    The closing professional verifies title, prepares documents, and pays you the full agreed amount at closing.

Who you’ll talk to

Our team

Our team works directly with property owners and home builders to help move vacant and underused land toward its next purpose.

Lauren and Hailey lead our property research and outreach. They identify properties that may be a good fit for what we and our builder partners are looking for and reach out directly to owners to start the conversation. If you’ve received a text, call, or email from Lauren or Hailey, they’re real members of our team reaching out because we’ve taken an interest in your property.

Joel leads acquisitions and works directly with property owners once there is interest in selling. He reviews potential purchases, discusses pricing and terms, answers questions about the process, and coordinates the transaction through closing. He also works with our network of builders and development partners to understand where they’re looking to build and what types of land they’re actively seeking.

We’re a virtual company, but we’re real people behind every conversation. We keep our team small intentionally so owners can communicate directly with the people working on their property rather than being passed around a large organization.

Whether you’re speaking with Lauren, Hailey, or Joel, you will receive the utmost care and respect for your transaction.
